Budget Notebook — Privacy Policy

Developer: CraftedByte  ·  Last updated: November 15, 2026

Short version: Budget Notebook is a fully offline app. All your records stay on your own phone. We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal or financial data.

1. What this app is

Budget Notebook (by CraftedByte) is a personal expense, income and loan (udhaar) tracker. It is designed to work completely offline, without any account or sign-in.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect any data. There is no server, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no tracking in this app. Specifically:

  • Your expenses, income, loan records, profile name/photo, and settings are saved only in a local database on your device.
  • We have no access to this data and it never leaves your device through us.
  • We do not collect usage statistics, device identifiers, or location.

3. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications — to show your daily reminder and due-date alerts. You can turn these off any time.
  • Biometric (fingerprint/face) — optional app lock. Your biometric data is handled entirely by your device's operating system; the app never sees or stores it.
  • Camera — only when you choose to attach a photo of a bill/receipt (slip). The photo is saved in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Save to gallery — only when you choose to save a slip photo to your phone's gallery.

4. Device backups

If you have enabled Android's own backup in your phone settings, Android may include app data in your device backup. This is managed entirely by Google/your device — CraftedByte has no access to it.

5. Sharing features

If you use the share feature (for example, sending your monthly summary via WhatsApp), the content you choose to share is sent by your device to the app you pick. We do not receive a copy.

6. Children's privacy

Budget Notebook does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because no data is collected at all.

7. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
