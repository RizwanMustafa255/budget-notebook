# Rozana Hisaab — Privacy Policy / Parivacy Policy

**Last updated: November 2026**

Rozana Hisaab ("we", "our", "the app") is developed by Rock Harry.

## English

**Your data never leaves your phone.**

Rozana Hisaab is a fully offline budget and udhaar (loan) tracker:

- **No data collection.** We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal or financial data on our servers. All entries (expenses, income, loans, profile name/photo) live only on your device's local storage.
- **No account required.** The app has no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts.
- **No internet access needed.** The app works completely offline.
- **No advertising.** The app contains no ads and no third-party ad/tracking SDKs.
- **No analytics or tracking.** We do not use analytics, crash reporters that send data, or any tracking technology.
- **Backups are yours.** If you export a backup (JSON/CSV), the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose (e.g., saving to Drive, sending via WhatsApp).
- **Optional permissions:**
  - *Biometric / fingerprint* — used only to unlock the app if you enable App Lock.
  - *Notifications* — used only for the daily reminder you schedule in the app.
- **Data deletion.** You can delete all data anytime from Settings → Data & Reset, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling removes everything permanently.

If you have questions, contact us at: **rozana.hisaab@gmail.com**

## Roman Urdu

Rozana Hisaab 100% offline app hai:

- Hum aap ka koi data (kharcha, kamai, udhaar, naam, photo) apne paas nahi rakhte — sab kuch sirf aap ke phone me rehta hai.
- Koi account nahi, koi login nahi, koi internet zaroori nahi.
- Koi advertisement nahi, koi tracking nahi.
- Backup aap khud banate hain (JSON/CSV) — wo file aap ke device par banti hai aur sirf wahan jahan aap bhejte hain.
- App Lock ke liye fingerprint sirf aap ke device par istemal hota hai.
- Data delete karna ho to Settings → Data & Reset, ya app uninstall kar dein — sab kuch hamesha ke liye mit jata hai.
